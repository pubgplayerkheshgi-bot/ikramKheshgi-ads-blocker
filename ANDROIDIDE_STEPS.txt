IKRAMKHESHKI POPUP AD DETECTOR — BUILD ON YOUR ANDROID PHONE

Recommended builder: AndroidIDE.

1. Install AndroidIDE on your Android phone.
2. Download this ZIP and extract it to a normal folder such as Download/IkramKheshgi.
3. Open AndroidIDE.
4. Choose Open/Import an existing Gradle project.
5. Select the extracted IkramKheshgi_PopupAdDetector folder.
6. Allow AndroidIDE to install/configure the required JDK and Android SDK/build tools when prompted.
7. Wait for Gradle sync to finish.
8. Tap the Run/Build button.
9. Choose the debug APK option if AndroidIDE asks.
10. Install the generated APK.
11. Open "ikramKheshgi Popup Detector".
12. Tap "ENABLE POPUP DETECTOR".
13. Android Settings > Accessibility > Installed services > ikramKheshgi Popup Detector > turn it ON.
14. Return to the app and use your phone normally.
15. Open Recent detections to see suspected app/window events.

IMPORTANT:
- The detector is best-effort. Android does not expose every advertising event to third-party apps.
- Accessibility access is powerful. This app is designed to process detection events locally and does not require internet access.
- Do not enable Accessibility access for apps you do not trust.

If AndroidIDE cannot sync:
- Make sure the phone has several GB of free storage.
- Keep the phone connected to Wi-Fi during the first Gradle/SDK setup.
- Restart AndroidIDE and try the Gradle sync again.
